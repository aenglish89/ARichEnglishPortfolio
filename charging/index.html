Where to charge your Kia Niro EV

The Niro EV takes two plugs. J1772 for home and public AC. CCS Combo 1 for DC fast charging. The port is on the front of the car.

Peak DC
85 kW
Usable pack
64.8 kWh
EPA range
253 miles

Three places to plug in

01

Home

Level 2, J1772

A wall charger at home is the easy daily habit. On an 11 kW Level 2 unit the pack fills in about 6 hours. A 7 kW unit takes about 9 hours. Plug in at night. Wake up full.

02

Public CCS

DC fast, CCS Combo 1

Use a public DC fast stall when you travel. The Niro takes CCS Combo 1 — the round face with two extra prongs. 10% to 80% takes about 45 minutes. Stop at 80%.

03

Tesla sites

Select Superchargers

Some Tesla Superchargers take other brands. You need a Magic Dock stall or a NACS adapter. Speed is still capped at 85 kW. Confirm the stall is open first.

How to charge your Kia Niro EV

Four steps. Same port every time. Unlock the car first. The charge door is on the nose.

01

Open the port

Unlock the car. Press the left side of the charge door. It swings open. The inlet is two sockets stacked: J1772 on top for home and public AC, CCS Combo 1 below for DC fast charging. Wipe away water or dirt before you plug in.

02

Plug in

Home or public AC: use the J1772 handle. Road-trip DC: use the CCS handle — a circle with two extra prongs on the bottom. Push straight in until it clicks. A green light on the port means the car accepted the plug. Line the stall up with the nose of the car before you park.

03

Start, then confirm it is charging

Start the session with the station app, an RFID card, or the button on the charger. Watch three places: the green bars on the port, the center screen, and the cluster behind the wheel. The cluster below shows a live session at 80%, 26 kW, and 50 minutes left to 100%. That taper is normal. On a trip, stop at 80% and go.

04

Unplug and close

Unlock the car to release the cable lock. Pull the handle straight out. Hang it on the stall. Press the charge door shut until it clicks. You are ready to drive.

Questions

How long does a charge take?
Level 2 at home: about 6 hours on an 11 kW charger, or about 9 hours on 7 kW. DC fast: about 45 minutes from 10% to 80%.
Can I use a Tesla Supercharger?
Yes, at sites that support other brands (Magic Dock) or with a NACS adapter. Speed is still capped at 85 kW.
How do I open the charge door?
Unlock the car. Press the left side of the door on the front of the vehicle. It pops open.
