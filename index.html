Good to know

Is this my first EV?

Good. The car shows range in miles, not percent. Plan on 210 highway miles and you will never be surprised.

Where do I charge on a road trip?

Any CCS fast charger. The Florida Turnpike service plazas have them. The charging guide lists the ones we use.

What does the charging cost?

Public fast charging runs about $0.45 per kWh, so a 10 to 80 percent stop is around $13.

Does the car need a special app?

The charging guide lists the two apps worth setting up before you leave.
